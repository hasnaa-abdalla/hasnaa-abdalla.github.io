Portrait ideas · scroll slowly

Something behind the picture

Five ways to place your portrait with a background that moves as the page scrolls. Each one stays in the paper-and-ink style of the site.

Idea 1 · Sketch

An old map drifts behind

A hand-drawn map of Alexandria (coastline, the Pharos, Canopic Way) slides and turns slowly as you scroll.

The sketch has its paper removed, so the map lines show through around you, like a drawing on top of an archive sheet.

Idea 2 · Photo

The arch draws itself

Your photo sits in an arched window, echoing the "HA" arch at the top of the site.

As you scroll, terracotta lines draw around it one after another, like ink being laid down with a pen.

Idea 3 · Sketch

The cities pass by

Large outlined names of the places in your story glide sideways behind you, each row in the opposite direction.

It tells the "life in a suitcase" story without a word of extra text.

Idea 4 · Photo

A mashrabiya screen

An eight-point star lattice, the kind carved into wooden window screens across Cairo, Alexandria and Doha, slides up slowly behind the photo.

The pattern fades out at the edges, so it frames you without crowding the photo.

Idea 5 · Both

Sketch turns into photo

Use both pictures: the drawing turns over like a page to reveal the photograph halfway down the scroll.

Behind it, a ring of words from your work rotates slowly, like a museum seal.
